# Acceptable use

FreeProxyAPI lists proxies that third parties have already published publicly
and measures whether they work. It does not scan networks or discover new
hosts.

This policy covers the hosted service and asks the same of anyone using the
software.

Do not use the service or the software to:

- attack, probe, or gain access to systems you are not authorized to use;
- scrape or access sites in violation of their terms, or evade bans, rate
  limits, or access controls;
- commit fraud, send spam, or distribute malware;
- generate high-volume traffic against third-party targets.

Never send credentials or sensitive data through a listed proxy: its operator
can read and modify unencrypted traffic, and some listed proxies are hostile.

The hosted API is rate limited per client IP, and abusive clients may be
blocked. Availability data is best effort and comes with no warranty.

To report abuse or ask for a listed endpoint or a feed source to be removed,
open an issue with the "Abuse report or removal request" template. If you own a
listed endpoint, say so and it will be removed.

See also [docs/responsible-use.md](docs/responsible-use.md) for operators and
[docs/privacy.md](docs/privacy.md).
