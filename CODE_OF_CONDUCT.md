# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free space for everyone,
whatever their experience, background, or identity.

## Our standards

Expected:

- being respectful and constructive, especially when you disagree;
- giving and accepting feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
- assuming good faith, and asking before assuming otherwise.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery;
- personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate derailing;
- publishing other people's private information without permission;
- sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct that would reasonably
  be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Because this project deals with proxies and network testing, requests for help
attacking, scanning, or accessing systems without authorization are also out of
bounds; see [ACCEPTABLE_USE.md](ACCEPTABLE_USE.md).

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when someone represents the project publicly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or
reject contributions that violate these standards, and may temporarily or
permanently ban a participant.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this
repository and begin the title with "Conduct:" so it is routed to the
maintainers rather than treated as a security issue. Reports are kept
confidential and handled fairly. A dedicated address will replace this when one
exists.

This text adapts the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
